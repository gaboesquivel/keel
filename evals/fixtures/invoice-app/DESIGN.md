# Design

Duesy is an operational tool people open briefly, often on a phone. It should feel calm and quick: the overdue list is the product.

- Use the tokens in `static/app.css` (`--accent`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--space`). The accent is reserved for actions and the brand.
- System font, 14px base, no illustrations or decorative imagery.
- Dense tables are fine on desktop; on narrow screens the list must remain usable without horizontal scrolling.
- Reminder actions must make it obvious that a reminder was sent and to whom.
