# Pilot notes, Alder Street

Pilot started 2026-10-13 (browsing); borrow requests live since 2026-10-27.

## Numbers (to 2026-11-17, counted by hand)

- 31 households signed up; 46 tools listed (24 in the app so far, the rest still on paper).
- 18 borrow requests sent through the app.
- 4 loans confirmed as handed over, all arranged in the street's WhatsApp group after the borrower asked there too.
- Returns are not tracked anywhere.

## What neighbours said

- Lenders: "I didn't know anyone had asked." Of the 18 requests, 9 were never seen by the lender until the borrower knocked or posted in the group. Nobody checks the Requests page unprompted.
- Borrowers: "I sent a request and heard nothing, so I bought one." (2 people)
- Nobody has asked for search. The list fits on two phone screens.
