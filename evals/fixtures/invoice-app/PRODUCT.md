# Duesy

## Purpose

Freelancers and two-to-five-person studios lose cash flow to invoices nobody chases. Duesy shows what is overdue and makes it one click to send a polite reminder, so they get paid sooner without awkward conversations.

## Primary user

A solo freelancer (designer, developer, consultant) with 5-40 active clients who invoices from another tool and wants follow-up handled. They check Duesy a few times a week, often on a phone between meetings.

## Value

Chasing is the part of invoicing people avoid. Accounting tools bury follow-up in settings; Duesy makes the overdue list the home screen.

## Primary metric

Share of invoices paid within 30 days of their due date. Measured weekly from payment records (`ops/metrics-weekly.csv`). Baseline at launch: 41%.

## Scope

- Overdue list with balances and last reminder
- One-click email reminders
- Manual invoice entry and CSV import of clients

## Non-goals

- Accounting, tax or bookkeeping: other tools do this well and freelancers already use one.
- Payment processing: we link to the freelancer's existing payment page instead of holding money.
- Team accounts and permissions: the user is one person; multi-user roles would double the product's surface.
