# Shelf

## Purpose

Most households buy tools they use once or twice a year. Shelf lets neighbours on one street or in one building lend the tools they already own, so a weekend job doesn't start with buying a drill.

## Primary user

Someone in a dense neighbourhood who needs a tool for a small job this week. Lenders are the same people: neighbours who own tools and are willing to lend them.

## Primary metric

Completed loans per month: a tool handed over and returned. Counted from loan records once they exist. Baseline: none yet (not launched).

## Scope

- Neighbours list tools they are willing to lend
- Find a tool nearby
- Ask to borrow it and arrange the handover
- Mark it returned

## Non-goals

- Payments, deposits or rental fees: lending is free, and money would change the relationship and bring payment handling and liability.
- Delivery or courier logistics: neighbours hand tools over in person.
- Public ratings of people: in a small community, star ratings create friction and fear rather than trust.

## Open questions

- Will lenders keep lending after the first tool comes back late or damaged?
